The Rule Room

One rule cracks dozens of words. Read the notes, then use the explorer to test it for yourself.

The two-faced letters

The letters c and g each make two sounds — a hard one and a soft one.

Remember: soft c = /s/ · soft g = /j/.

The e-i-y rule

It’s the next letter that decides. A vowel e, i or y makes c and g go soft.

Remember: refugee, dangerous and courageous all have a soft g — there’s an e right after it.
