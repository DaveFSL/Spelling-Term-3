Root Lab

Read the root toolkit, then tap a word to break it into its parts and see what each one means.

stat- = stand / state

From the idea of facts that stand firm: statistics, statistic, statistician.

Remember: statistics make data stand up as facts.

per- + cent-

per = for each · cent = hundred. So per cent means “for each hundred”, and % is a part out of 100.

Remember: percentage = a score out of one hundred.

Suffix signposts

-tion/-ation a process-ity a state-ical relating to
Remember: the suffix tells you the word’s job.
