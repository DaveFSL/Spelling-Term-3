Case Notes

Four spelling strategies for the long eco words. Use them whenever a giant word turns up.

1 · Hear the hidden “n”

Many people drop the middle n and write “enviroment”. Say en·vi·RON·ment and you’ll hear it.

Remember: environment has a hidden n in the middle.

2 · Double trouble: ll

pollute already has a double l, and it stays in pollution. Don’t drop one.

Remember: pollution keeps the double l.

3 · -tion sounds like /shun/

Three of our words end in -tion: conservation, vegetation, pollution. It sounds like /shun/.

Remember: /shun/ at the end = t-i-o-n.

4 · Chunk the giants

For a giant like sustainability, tap the beats: sus·tain·a·bil·i·ty, then spell each chunk.

Remember: chunk it into beats, spell each beat, join them up.

Crack the Case

Prove the spellings in real writing. Write 2–3 sentences about protecting an environment, using at least three focus words spelt correctly. Underline each one.

Focus words: environment · sustainability · ecosystem · biodiversity · conservation · vegetation · pollution · climate

Sentence starters

• Reducing pollution protects the environment because …
• Conservation of our vegetation helps …
• A healthy ecosystem needs …
• Looking after biodiversity matters because …
