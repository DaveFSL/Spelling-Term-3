Case Notes

Four facts for your eco notebook. Learn these and the long words stop being scary.

1 · bio- = life

bio- means life. It starts words about living things: biodiversity, biology, biosphere.

Remember: see bio-, think life.

2 · eco- = home / environment

eco- comes from the Greek for home. It links geography and science: ecosystem, ecology.

Remember: eco- = the home living things share.

3 · de- = remove / take away

The prefix de- means remove, down or undo: deforestation, degrade, decompose.

Remember: de- takes something away.

4 · -tion & -ity build nouns

-tion turns a word into a process (pollution, conservation). -ity makes a quality (sustainability).

Remember: -tion = a process · -ity = a quality.
03

Root Explorer

Tap a root to reveal what it means and the words it builds. You’ll need these meanings in the Forge.

bio- → life. Anything to do with living things.

Builds: biodiversity (variety of life), biology (study of life), biosphere (the zone where life exists).

eco- → home / environment. From the Greek oikos, meaning house.

Builds: ecosystem (living things + their home), ecology (study of how living things share a home).

de- → remove / down / undo. It reverses or reduces something.

Builds: deforestation (removing forests), degrade (break down), decompose (rot down into parts).
