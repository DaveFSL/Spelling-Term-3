02

Strategy Notes

Three moves that shrink any long word down to size.

Chunk it into beats

Say the word slowly and clap each beat. Spell one chunk at a time.

Remember: 4 small chunks are easier than 1 long word.

Spot the ending

Many long words share an ending: -graphy (topography), -tude (latitude), -sion (erosion).

Remember: learn the ending once, reuse it everywhere.

Say it to spell it

Pronounce every syllable on purpose — pe·nin·su·la — even the quiet ones. Your voice guides your pen.

Remember: if you can say the beats, you can spell the word.
