Theme Writing: Spelling in Context

Write 2–3 sentences about how a character shows courage or finds belonging. Use at least three focus words — the counter tracks them for you.

Focus words used: 0 / 3 minimum

Sentence starters

· It took great ________ for the character to ________.

· Even after losing their home, the family showed ________ by ________.

· A sense of ________ finally came when ________.

· The strangers treated them with ________, which meant ________.

Fill the gaps with focus words from the Word Wall, then finish each idea in your own words.

One way it could look

Leaving home took enormous courage, but the children faced their displacement with quiet resilience. When a stranger finally showed them compassion, they felt the first flicker of belonging in their new sanctuary.

That uses six focus words — yours only needs three. Check each of yours against the Word Wall spelling.
