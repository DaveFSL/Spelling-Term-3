Strategy Notes

Three quick notes from the case file before you start hunting.

What’s a double letter?

Two of the same letter side by side: satellite, constellation, beginning. It’s easy to drop one by accident.

Remember: if it sounds long or stressed, check for a double.

Say it slowly

Break the word into beats and stretch each one: sat·el·lite, con·stel·la·tion. The double often hides right where two beats join.

Remember: slow your voice down and your pen will catch the double.
