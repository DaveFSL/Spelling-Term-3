Case Notes — quick recall

A 20-second refresher on the traps before you write and test. Glance, then go.

statistics — sta·tis·tics. Two t-i pairs.
frequency — keep the q-u team: fre·quen·cy.
percentage — per + cent + age.
estimation — ends -tion, said /shun/.
probability — -ity = a quality; prob·a·bil·i·ty.
categorical — -ical, not “-icle”.
average — av·er·age (3 beats).
variable — var·i·a·ble; ends -able.

04

Field Report

Write up the survey. In 2–3 sentences, describe what the graph shows. Use at least three focus words and underline each one.

Focus words: statistics · frequency · percentage · average · estimation · variable · categorical · probability

Sentence starters

• Our statistics showed that …
• The highest frequency was for …
• A large percentage of students chose …
• Favourite game is categorical data because …

Cloze — fill the gaps:

Our ______ showed that handball had the highest ______. A large ______ of the class picked it. Favourite game is ______ data because it sorts into groups.

Tap a word to drop it into your report.

🔒 Write at least one sentence of your own first — then the worked example unlocks.
Worked example (one way to do it):
Our statistics showed that handball had the highest frequency, chosen by a large percentage of the class. Favourite game is categorical data, and the average bar height was about nine students. That uses five focus words. Yours only needs three — check each against your Case Notes.
