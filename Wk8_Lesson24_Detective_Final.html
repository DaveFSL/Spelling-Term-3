03

Cross-Subject Sentences

Use at least three -ise (or -yse) verbs in science, maths or HASS contexts — like a real report would. The counter tracks your verbs.

-ise family verbs used: 0 / 3 minimum

Sentence starters

· Scientists ________ their results before ________. (science)

· In maths, we ________ the data into groups so that ________. (maths)

· Historians ________ that the event happened because ________. (HASS)

· Before the test, I ________ my notes by ________. (any subject)

Fill the gaps with verbs from the -ise family: organise, recognise, summarise, analyse, hypothesise, categorise…

One way it could look

Scientists analyse their results before they hypothesise about the cause. In maths we organise survey data into a table, then summarise what it shows in one clear sentence.

That's four -ise family verbs across two subjects — yours needs three. Check each ending: s, not z (and analyse keeps its y).
