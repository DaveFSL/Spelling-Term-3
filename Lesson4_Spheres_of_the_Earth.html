“ph” says /f/

In atmosphere and hydrosphere, the letters ph make the /f/ sound — not “f”.

Remember: sphere is spelt s-p-h-e-r-e.

Two odd ones out

rotation = rotate + -ion (the spinning). gravity = from Latin gravis, “heavy”. No “-sphere” here!

Remember: not every Earth word is built from -sphere.
