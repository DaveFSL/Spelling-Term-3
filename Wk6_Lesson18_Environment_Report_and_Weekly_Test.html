Case Notes — quick recall

A 20-second refresher on this week’s traps before you proofread, write and test. Glance, then go.

environment — hidden n: en·vi·ron·ment.
sustainability — sus·tain·a·bil·i·ty, ends -ity.
ecosystem — eco (home) + system.
biodiversity — bio (life) + diversity.
conservation — ends -tion, said /shun/.
vegetation — veg·e·ta·tion, -tion ending.
pollution — double l, ends -tion.
climate — ends -ate, not -it.

04

Field Report

Now write your own. In 2–3 sentences, explain how we can protect an environment. Use at least three focus words, spelt correctly, and underline each one.

Focus words: environment · sustainability · ecosystem · biodiversity · conservation · vegetation · pollution · climate

Sentence starters

• We can protect this environment by …
• Reducing pollution helps the ecosystem because …
• Conservation of the vegetation matters because …
• Looking after biodiversity keeps …

Cloze — fill the gaps:

We can protect the ______ by reducing ______. Caring for the ______ supports its ______, and ______ projects keep the area healthy for the future.

Tap a word to drop it into your writing box.

🔒 Write at least one sentence of your own first — then the worked example unlocks.
Worked example (one way to do it):
We can protect this environment by cutting pollution and supporting conservation. Healthy vegetation keeps the local ecosystem in balance for the future. That uses five focus words. Yours only needs three — check each against your Case Notes.
